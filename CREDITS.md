# Credits

All music in this game comes from [ccMixter](https://ccmixter.org) and is used under Creative Commons licences. No other audio is included.

Changes made to every track: a 30-second section was cut out, each stem was mixed down to mono and re-encoded as 96 kbps MP3, and some stems were combined (listed below). While you play, the game changes the mix live (muting, level, EQ, panning, reverb and clipping). These changes are made by the game, not by the artists.
